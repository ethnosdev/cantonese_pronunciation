# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the **Cantonese Pronunciation** mobile application (`dev.ethnos.cantonese`).

## Data Collection

**Cantonese Pronunciation does not collect, track, sell, or share any personal data.**

- **No accounts or sign-in:** You do not create an account or provide a name, email address, or any personal details.
- **No analytics, ads, or tracking:** The app contains no advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, crash-reporting telemetry, or third-party data collection.
- **No sensitive device permissions:** The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos. Audio features are playback-only.

## Local On-Device Storage

Your learning progress, review schedule, pronunciation practice statistics, app settings (such as theme and practice filters), and the downloaded pronunciation audio bundle are stored **only on your device**. You can reset statistics and progress or clear settings within the app, or remove all local data by uninstalling the app.

## Network Requests and External Links

- **Audio bundle download:** On initial setup (or if you choose to repair/re-download the bundle), the app makes a standard HTTPS `GET` request to `https://cantonese.ethnos.dev/kt_audio_opus_v1.zip` to download the offline audio corpus. Standard server transport metadata (such as your IP address and request timestamp) may be processed temporarily by the hosting server to deliver the file. Once installed, pronunciation drills run offline.
- **External links:** The About & Credits screen provides optional links to third-party sites (`jyut.net`, Creative Commons, and GitHub) that open in your device's web browser under their own privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

## Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices change in a future update, this policy will be updated accordingly with a revised effective date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please use the developer support contact listed on the Apple App Store or Google Play store listing for **Cantonese Pronunciation** (`dev.ethnos.cantonese`).
